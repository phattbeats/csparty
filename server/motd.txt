CS PARTY  -  Mario Party rules, Counter-Strike everything.

Most stars at the end wins. /party starts, /board picks the map.



YOUR TURN: pick "Open a case" from the menu. The reel does the rest.

Menus: number keys, or up/down + JUMP to pick, USE to go back.

Buy gear for the minigames. Survive and keep it, die and lose it.



BOARD: blue pays you, red costs you, ? is an event.

Black Market and Armory: items. Duel: 1v1 for cash.



STARS: reach the hostages and pay $5000. TAB shows the stars.

MINIGAMES: after everyone moves.

Esc (Start on a controller, Menu on a phone): settings.
