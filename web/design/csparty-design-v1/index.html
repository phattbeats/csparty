Controls & how to play

On your turn, Jump selects “Jump at the crate”. Jump again rolls. Use number keys, or move up/down and Jump to pick; Use goes back. Keyboard: Space / W / S / E. Controller: stick or D-pad, A, B. Touch: on-screen stick. Type /help in chat for the rules. Click the game to capture the mouse; Esc opens the menu.
