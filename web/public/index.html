Controls & how to play

On your turn, Jump picks "Jump at the crate", Jump again rolls. Menus: number keys, or move up/down and Jump to pick, Use to go back. Keyboard: Space / W / S / E. Controller: stick or D-pad, A, B. Touch: on-screen stick. Type /help in chat for the rules. Click the game to capture the mouse; Esc opens the menu (Start on a controller, the Menu button on a phone).
