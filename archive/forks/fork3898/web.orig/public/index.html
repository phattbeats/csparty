CS Party

Mario Party rules, Counter-Strike everything. You're joining the real server from your browser.

On a phone: play in landscape. Left thumb moves (and steers the menus), Jump picks and jumps. Graphics are turned down to run smoothly. A Bluetooth controller works too. Tip: add this page to your home screen for full screen.
First visit downloads about 45 MB of game data. After that it's saved in your browser.
Your turn: Jump picks "Jump at the crate", Jump again rolls. Menus: number keys, or move up/down and Jump to pick, Use to go back. Works with Space/W/S/E, a controller (stick or D-pad, A, B) or the touch stick. /help in chat for the rules. Click the game to capture the mouse, Esc for the menu.
