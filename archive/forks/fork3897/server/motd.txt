CS PARTY  -  Mario Party rules, Counter-Strike everything.
Most stars at the end wins. Say /party to start.

CONTROLS
 Menus: number keys, or move up/down + JUMP to pick, USE to go back.
 Keyboard: W/S Space E   Controller: stick/D-pad, A, B   Phone: left thumb, Jump
 Your turn: JUMP picks "Jump at the crate", JUMP again rolls.

YOUR TURN
 Jump at the crate: roll.
 Buy gear: the real buy menu. Gear goes into the minigames.
   Survive and keep it. Die and lose it.
 Use items: Knife Out, Fake Call, C4, Rotate...

THE BOARD
 Blue: pays you, CT side.  Red: costs you, T side.  ?: event
 Black Market: items at T and CT spawn. Armory: free item
 Camper: an AWPer takes a cut. Smoke saves you
 Duel: 1v1 for cash. VIP Escort: slot reel. Negotiator: buy cash or a star

STARS
Reach the hostages and pay $5000. Stars are your TAB score.
Board money is your CS money.

MINIGAMES
After everyone moves. Some are on their own map (surf, bhop).
